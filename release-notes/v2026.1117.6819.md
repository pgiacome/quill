Quill is an unofficial Telegram client and still in beta.

Four of the changes below came from contributors. Thank you to `@sprainbrains`, `@pgiacome` and `@abalakh`. Their commits are kept with their names on them.

### New

- Quill is now fully translated into Russian (#1477, from #1468 by `@sprainbrains`). It covers every part of the interface Quill draws itself, with proper Russian plurals, and the menus switch language along with the rest of the app.
- The Calls tab lists group calls and live streams that are running in your chats, above Recent calls, with a Join button (#1475, from #1469 by `@sprainbrains`). A group call update no longer opens or closes the call window on its own.
- Compact mode, in Settings › Appearance, fits more chats and messages on screen with smaller avatars, shorter rows and a tighter header and composer (#1467, from #1463 by `@abalakh`).
- Topic rows in a forum look like chat-list rows: the topic icon, name and time, then the sender and the last message, with unread, mention and reaction counters on the right (#1476, from #1470 by `@sprainbrains`). Custom emoji topic icons now show.

### Improvements

- Submenus stay open while you move the pointer diagonally toward them, as in Telegram Desktop (#1479). This covers chat, profile, attach and formatting menus, and the menu bar on Linux and Windows.
- After you pick an in-chat search result, the results list folds away so the chat is in view (#1466). The "N of M" counter shows the list again, and Esc folds it before closing the search.
- Spelling underlines are drawn with the text, so they stay on the right word while you type (#1480).

### Fixes

- On X11 desktops that don't set a DPI, such as Xfce, MATE and LXQt at their defaults, Quill now opens at the desktop's scale instead of looking much too large (#1474, from #1471 by `@pgiacome`).
- A reduced-motion choice made by Power Saving or battery saving now stays in effect (#1480).

### Reliability

- GPUI Kit 0.10 and this month's dependency updates (#1480).

### Not yet tested on a live account

- These changes were checked with tests and offline demos, not on a live account. Compact mode's window buttons on macOS were checked by reading the code only.
