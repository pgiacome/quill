Quill is an unofficial Telegram client and still in beta.

### Translations

- Much more of Quill now follows your language (#1484 by `@sprainbrains`, with review fixes in #1487). Service messages (joins, pins, title and photo changes, gifts, giveaways, boosts, video chats and the rest) now use Telegram's own wording, so they are translated in every language Telegram supports. Reply bars, comment and reply counts, "seen" times, the edited and imported labels, the Replies chat title, birthday cards, the giveaway box, media viewer timers, shared-media empty pages, link errors and screen-reader labels are translated too. Russian gets full translations of the Quill-only lines.
- The Replies chat title now changes as soon as you switch language, on every signed-in account.
- Russian plurals are right for amounts of 1,000 and up, such as Grams and kilometres.
- The giveaway box shows bold text as bold instead of raw `**` marks.

### Not yet tested on a live account

- The new translations were checked with tests and offline demos, not on a live account.
